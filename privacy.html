Privacy Policy

Inkpaper (iOS) · Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Inkpaper (“the App”) handles information. The App is a document scanning and e-signature tool for iPhone.

1. Information we process

  • Documents & images you create — photos from the camera, imported photos/PDFs, signatures, and exported PDFs. These stay on your device unless you choose to share/export them.
  • Purchase status — whether you unlocked Inkpaper Pro (handled by Apple StoreKit). We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Diagnostics & usage analytics — crash reports and aggregated product events (for example: scan completed, paywall viewed, purchase restored) via Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (Google). These may include device identifiers and app interaction data. They are not used to show personalized ads and we do not sell your data.

2. Permissions

  • Camera — to scan documents.
  • Photo Library — when you import images.

You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings.

3. How we use information

  • Provide core App features (scan, sign, export, organize).
  • Unlock Pro features after a verified Apple purchase/restore.
  • Improve stability and understand which features are used (analytics/crash reporting).

4. Data storage & sharing

Your documents and signatures are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a user account system or cloud sync for your files. When you use Share/Export, content leaves the device only through the iOS share sheet or apps you choose.

Analytics/crash data is processed by Google Firebase under their terms and privacy policy.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Do not use the App if you are under 13.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy: replace with your support email before shipping (example: support@yourdomain.com).

Chính sách quyền riêng tư

Inkpaper (iOS) · Cập nhật: 10/09/2026

Inkpaper là ứng dụng quét tài liệu và ký trên iPhone.

1. Dữ liệu xử lý

  • Tài liệu & ảnh bạn tạo — ảnh camera, ảnh/PDF import, chữ ký, PDF xuất. Lưu trên máy trừ khi bạn chủ động chia sẻ.

2. Quyền truy cập

  • Camera — quét tài liệu.
  • Thư viện ảnh — khi import ảnh.

3. Mục đích

Cung cấp tính năng quét/ký/xuất; xác nhận mua Pro; cải thiện độ ổn định và hiểu mức dùng tính năng.

4. Lưu trữ & chia sẻ

Tài liệu lưu cục bộ trên thiết bị. Không có tài khoản đồng bộ cloud trong App. Khi Share/Export, nội dung chỉ rời máy qua hệ thống chia sẻ iOS hoặc app bạn chọn.
